The road to the tour

Arcade golf

Two-click swing, draw and fade, wind, sloping greens and backspin. Ryan's skills change how every shot feels, on six courses from the home muni to Highland Ridge.

A career RPG

Train, work shifts, rest and see friends each week. Story events, a rival, sponsors and money to manage, from the Amateur Circuit through Q-School to the LPGA.

Your clubhouse

Move up from a one-bed apartment above a taco shop to the Grand Clubhouse. Decorate it and fill the cabinets with every trophy you win.

Madame Monique's wheel

Roulette at Desert Palms, run by a croupier who remembers every streak, every big win and how your golf is going. The wheel remembers, darling.

Desert Palms Casino

Blackjack with Velvet Vega, slots, and craps with the stickman, for a night off the course. In-game money only.

Greyhound racing

Thirty-six dogs, a new field of six every race, and odds worked out from how they match up. Back one to win and watch it run.
