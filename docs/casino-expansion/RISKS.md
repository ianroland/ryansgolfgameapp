# Risk register (revision 2)

Scoring: L = likelihood, I = impact (each High, Medium or Low).

| # | Risk | L | I | Mitigation | Owner / gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Sportsbook infrastructure doesn't exist.** The game is a static Netlify site with local saves (audit §1). The bible requires secrets held on a server, a licensed feed and an authoritative ledger. | H | H | Treat it as a separate project at SB0: provider shortlist, costs, rate limits, licensing terms for display in a game, hosting (functions + DB), monitoring. Ship it last. Simulated league clearly labelled as a fallback. | You, SB0 |
| R2 | **Licensing and legal for real-data betting**, even with fictional money: provider terms often forbid use in games; app-store and age-rating rules apply to simulated gambling; some jurisdictions are sensitive. | M | H | Legal and platform review before choosing a provider. Keep it out of real-money mechanics (already canon). Age gate if required. | You, SB0 |
| R3 | **Provider cost and rate limits.** Odds feeds can be expensive and per-call. | M | M | A server-side cache with scheduled ingestion. Clients never call the provider. Budget cap with alerts. | SB1 |
| R4 | **Identity for server tickets.** There are no accounts. An anonymous install id can be lost or copied with a save. | H | M | Idempotent ticket ids. Tickets are tied to an install plus a per-save secret. Accept that fictional money can't be fully tamper-proof, or add accounts (a larger scope). | SB0 |
| R5 | **Rules correctness** (Omaha 2+3, Hi-Lo splits and quartering, side pots, no-limit raise rules, Stud bring-in). | M | H | Pure engines with exhaustive and table-driven tests, as done for Three Card Poker and Baccarat (hand-count tables, edge-check simulations). Variant rules signed off (Q6) before coding. | P1–P4 |
| R6 | **Persistent wagering state.** A reload or a second tab mid-hand, mid-match or mid-ticket could double-charge or lose a stake. | M | H | Save-before-animate; journals with seeds; idempotency keys per the save plan; resume tests for every flow. Known pre-existing gap: blackjack mid-hand (audit §5). Don't copy it. | All money gates |
| R7 | **Save migration (v2 to v3).** A bad upgrade could corrupt saves or unlock wrongly. | L | H | One pure, idempotent upgrade function shared by Dexie and file import. Fixture saves. Unlocks backfilled only from ledger facts. The older-build refusal is documented. | F1 |
| R8 | **Likeness rights.** Real PGA/LPGA players, logos or tournaments. | M | H | Fictional pros only (canon). Review names to avoid accidental resemblance. | Art B, scripts |
| R9 | **Asset quality and consistency.** Generated sheets have had baked labels and cards, keyed-out hair, mismatched canvases, and static poses mislabelled as animation (seen in 0.14.0 intake). | H | M | `ASSET_SPEC.md` rules; the per-batch QA checklist; in-game inspection at target size before acceptance; no production use of concept sheets. | Art A–F |
| R10 | **Mixed pixel densities** (cast atlases vs casino dealers). | M | M | Q12 decision; cast-scale sets for NPCs that speak in encounters. | Art B/C |
| R11 | **Scope creep.** The expansion is roughly the size of the existing game. | H | H | Vertical slices with gates; the sportsbook is isolated; no gate starts without its open questions answered. | You |
| R12 | **Economy balance.** Both unlocks count every income source [CONFIRMED], so a lucky casino run can open White Buffalo ($100K) early. | M | M | Keep every house game negative-expectation (already true and tested). Size skins pots to golf earnings. Simulate typical careers to see when $100K and $1M arrive. If White Buffalo opens before its content ships, show "opening soon". | Tuning, each gate |
| R13 | **Dialogue spam and pacing.** | M | L | At most one event per visit; never mid-deal; quiet mode (already in 0.14.0); cooldowns. | F5 |
| R14 | **Performance and bundle size.** The bundle is already 2.36 MB (Vite warns). New art and poker AI add more. | M | L | Lazy-load location and poker chunks; WebP for backgrounds; atlases. | Each gate |
| R15 | **Story is gated at Act I** (`story.act` never advances). Content gated on acts would never appear. | H | M | Expansion uses its own triggers (Q3). | F5 |
