## 1. Character identity

**ESTABLISHED CHARACTER DIRECTION** ([BR], [SD] decisions, [ART])

- **Name:** Baba. He has no first name; [SD] records the author's decision and he is "Baba" everywhere.
- **Relationship:** Ryan's father.
- **Background:** Lebanese-American, about 60. In Scene 3 he says "I'm sixty-one".
- **Occupation:** For thirty years, head groundskeeper at the municipal course ("the muni") where Ryan learned golf.
- **Career arc:**
  - In Act I he is still head groundskeeper.
  - From Act III he is officially retired.
  - He still mows the muni's practice green at 5 a.m., because "the new man cuts it too long".
  - His line for it: "Nobody told the grass."
  - The brief ([BR]) sets the arc; the wording is from [SD]. The arc is established; the line itself is DRAFT.
- **Role:** One of the game's emotional anchors, and Ryan's primary family relationship. He is not a supporting prop: he has his own life and agency (§3).

## 2. Visual design

**ESTABLISHED CHARACTER DIRECTION** ([BR], [ART]). Do not redesign without approval.

- **Hair:** Horseshoe balding: a bald crown with grey-black sides.
- **Face:** A signature moustache.
- **Clothes:** A cream golf polo with navy trim and a small cedar emblem, plus khaki slacks.
- **Watch:** A gold wristwatch. He checks it impatiently and times things with it.
- **No cap and no stopwatch.** The [FC] first draft of Scene 1 had a cap gesture; [SD] removed it.
- **Cedar:** The cedar is an emblem, a respectful nod to Lebanon. Don't use the full flag. ([FC]; DRAFT recommendation, consistent with [ART].)

## 4. Personality and humour

**ESTABLISHED CHARACTER DIRECTION** ([BR] §2, [FC] guardrails)

- Blunt, demanding and intensely competitive.
- Stubborn and practical.
- An expert in course maintenance.
- Unintentionally hilarious.
- Fiercely protective of Ryan.
- Emotionally reserved and deeply loving.

**Writing rules** ([BR], [FC] guardrail 4):

- His humour comes from his character: a groundskeeper's eye and a coach's bluntness, never his ethnicity.
- No phonetic accent. His English is written straight.
- Cultural detail is specific and light: BOBO MILKY, and the occasional *habibi* (phase 1) or *habibti* (phase 3).
- He is funny, never sentimental. He uses jokes and practical help where other people would make speeches.

**Running gags** ([FC], [SD]; DRAFT):

1. **Ball marks.** He counts everyone's unrepaired ball marks and always knows Biff's exact total.
2. **The gold watch.** He checks it whenever anyone wastes his time, usually Biff.
3. **The straw.** Ryan can never free the tiny straw under pressure; Baba does it in one move.
4. **Aurum-X against BOBO MILKY.** See §9.

## 5. Relationship with Ryan

**ESTABLISHED CHARACTER DIRECTION** ([BR] §3, [FC] guardrails 1–3)

- He wants Ryan to be the best golfer possible.
- He may not understand Ryan's transition or circumstances, but his love is unconditional and never depends on winning or on who she is.
- **His confusion is never cruelty.** It is about the situation (the tour, the rules, the secrecy), never about whether she deserves love.
- In phase 3 he uses she/her. Misgendering is never a joke.
- He criticizes her putting stroke, never her identity.
- He never decides for her, including about filing or disclosure.
- He shows love through practical things:
  - advice, fixing equipment and practising with her;
  - reading greens and BOBO MILKY;
  - protecting her from outsiders;
  - remembering her childhood ambition.
- **Philosophy:** "Ryan, I don't understand half of what you're doing. But if you're going to do it, you better win."
  - The theme is ESTABLISHED: [BR], and it is printed on [ART].
  - Using it as a production line is **DRAFT**.
  - It appears in [SD] Scene 1 with "A man your age should have a job with a parking space" between the two halves.
  - It appears in [SD] Scene 3 verbatim.

## 6. Relationship with Mom

**ESTABLISHED CHARACTER DIRECTION** ([BR] §4, [FC] guardrail 5)

- Ryan and Mom are distant. No reason is given, and none is to be invented.
- Mom is never a villain, never cruel, never mocked, and never contrasted unkindly with Baba.
- She doesn't disappear. Preserve existing approved Mom scenes unless an amendment is authorized.

**DRAFT** ([FC], [SD]):

- She appears rarely and briefly: close call 10, "Mom at the Major" (kept, with a rewritten opening), plus occasional texts or cards.
- Her warm Act II beats move to Baba as new scenes.

**Pending: the family amendment.** It is not authorized, so the frozen Bible and these drafts disagree about Mom's role.

**IMPLEMENTED IN GAME:** a warm, involved Mom. See the continuity report §2.

## 7. Relationship with Biff Fairway III

**ESTABLISHED CHARACTER DIRECTION** ([BR] §5)

- Don't change Biff's established personality. In the game he believes expensive equipment is the same as talent, and he calls Ryan "muni kid".

**DRAFT** ([SD] Scene 2):

- Baba has contempt for Biff's carelessness, above all his unrepaired ball marks.
- He calls Biff "the Fairway boy".
- He refuses every offer for a carton.
- He teaches Biff to repair a ball mark with a broken wooden tee after Biff's solid-gold repair tool folds flat.

## 13. Continuity rules

1. Baba's love never depends on results or identity, in any scene or any branch.
2. His confusion is about the situation, never about her worth. She/her in phase 3, always. Misgendering is never a joke.
3. He criticizes her putting, not her identity.
4. Ryan's choices are hers. He never decides for her.
5. No phonetic accent and no ethnic humour.
6. Mom is distant, never a villain. Don't invent a reason for the distance.
7. No cap and no stopwatch. He checks the gold watch.
8. No first name.
9. The coffee can stays on the shelf in his maintenance shed until The Rejection.
10. BOBO MILKY is affection, never a punchline, and makes no performance or health claims.
11. Aurum-X is Biff's. It has no effect for the player and is never sold to Ryan.
12. Biff's personality doesn't change.
13. No Baba scene changes Heat, knowledge, caper clues, golf physics, tournament eligibility, career-phase progression or Pink Jacket eligibility.
14. Scene 3's green-reading gives the player no usable information. [SD] removed "breaks to the lake".
15. Locked scripts (The Rejection, The Arnold Palmer Drive, The Dossier) change only with explicit authorization.
